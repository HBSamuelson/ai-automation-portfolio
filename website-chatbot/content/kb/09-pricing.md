# Pricing

## Posted fees

Posted fees, before insurance, are $189 for a new patient exam and cleaning, $129 for an adult cleaning, $99 for a child cleaning, $399 for take-home whitening trays, $220 and up for a tooth-colored filling, and $150 for an emergency exam.

## Quotes

Prices on this page are office fees before insurance. A coordinator confirms your portion before treatment. BrightSmile does not guarantee an insurance payment.
