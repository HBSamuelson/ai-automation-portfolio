# Emergency visits

## Same-day exam

An emergency exam is $150 before any treatment. Call (303) 555-0140 if a tooth hurts and you need to be seen the same day. Treatment that same day is quoted after the exam.

## Limits

The website assistant cannot tell you whether a tooth is infected or which medicine to use. Come in for an exam, or call 911 if you cannot breathe or the swelling is spreading fast.
